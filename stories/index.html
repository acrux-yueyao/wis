我一直描述不出来的东西是……
The thing I can never describe is…

观众接着这句话写下的答案，和一台机器对它们的再读。画面的风格来自展厅里的十一段影像；它们不是故事本身，是猜测——和你脑中那幅一定不一样。
Answers written by visitors, and a machine's second reading of each. The images borrow the style of the eleven films; they are guesses, not the stories — and never the picture in your head.

墙上还是空的——去写下第一条。 / The wall is still empty — go write the first one.
