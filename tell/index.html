我一直描述不出来的东西是……

The thing I can never describe is…

这是我们问卷里的一道题。之前有三十四个人填过：有人写雨来之前的气味，有人写妈妈叫自己名字的方式，有人只写了两个字，真心。
你也写一个。写完以后，我们会让一个只看过这十一段影像的程序试着把它画出来。它多半会画错。没关系，画错的我们也一起挂出来。

This is a question from our survey. Thirty-four people have answered it so far: the smell before rain, the way a mother says your name, one person just wrote "sincerity".
Write yours. Afterwards a program that has only ever seen the eleven films will try to draw it. It will probably get something wrong. That's fine, we hang the wrong ones too.

每一条都是人先看过，再交给程序；程序画两张，我们挑一张，旁边写明是机器猜的。不问姓名。写到别人隐私的不会放出来。
A person reads every entry before the program does. The program draws two pictures, we pick one, and it is labelled as a machine's guess. We don't ask for names. Anything that gives away another person's private life stays off the wall.
